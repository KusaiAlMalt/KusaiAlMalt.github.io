LIVBOJ

Academic project conducted at Chalmers University of Technology in collaboration with IKEA with focus on sustainability.

One of IKEAs sustainability goals is that each and every co-worker should report 2 incidents per year. Currently for workers it takes too much time to send in a report resulting in some reports lacking in quality or some incidents not being reported at all. How can this be fixed? With LIVBOJ, a new reporting system that implements AI, workers and managers are going to spend less filling out forms thus motivating them to report more often and more properly. And freeing up time for more important analysis!

Net Zero Calculator (NZC)

Academic project conducted at Chalmers University of Technology with focus on Agile.

The Net Zero Calculator is an AI-powered solution that provides your company with early estimation of what it takes to offset your organization's carbon footprint. We analyze your day-to-day emissions and connect you with verified carbon capture projects worldwide, making sustainability both accessible and actionable.
